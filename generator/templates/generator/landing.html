How it works

Three steps, one afternoon.

01

Bring what you have

Upload an existing resume PDF or paste plain text. CVAI reads it into structured fields — contact details, roles, dates, bullets, education, skills — that you can edit directly.

Two-column and sidebar layouts are reconstructed in reading order, not left-to-right across the page.

02

Aim it at one job

Paste the posting you are actually applying to. CVAI compares it against your resume, shows which requirements go unanswered, and rewrites the bullets that matter for that role.

One tailored resume per posting beats one general resume sent everywhere — which is the whole reason the generation count matters.

03

Export and keep track

Pick a template, watch the page redraw as you type, and download a PDF built from real text. Then log where you sent it and what came back.

The preview uses the same renderer as the download, so the file you send is the page you approved.

FAQ

Before you start.

How does this get past an ATS?
The PDF is built from real text rather than an image of a resume, so it can be selected and searched. Every template keeps a single text column underneath the visual layout, which means the screening software reads your sections in the order you wrote them instead of interleaving two columns into one scrambled block.
Can I upload the resume I already have?
Yes. Upload a PDF or paste plain text and CVAI reads it into editable fields — contact details, roles, dates, bullets, education and skills. Two-column and sidebar resumes are reconstructed in reading order rather than straight across the page.
Will it make up numbers about my career?
No. The rewriter is instructed to restructure only what you gave it. If your bullet contains no metric, the rewrite will sharpen the verb and the specifics you did provide and leave the number out — it will not fill the gap with a plausible-looking percentage. Everything on your resume has to be something you can defend in the interview.
What counts as one generation?
One AI request: generating a resume, writing a cover letter, running a keyword gap check, or rewriting a section. Editing your text, switching templates, previewing and downloading the PDF are all free and unlimited on every plan.
What is the XYZ formula?
A widely used pattern for writing resume bullets: accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]. It forces each line to name a result and its evidence rather than a responsibility. CVAI's rewriter follows it — within the limits of what your own input actually says.
Can I cancel?
Yes, from the Stripe billing portal linked in your profile, with no cancellation fee. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

No question matches that. Try “PDF”, “ATS”, or “cancel”.
