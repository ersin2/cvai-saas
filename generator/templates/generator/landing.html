FAQ

Before you start.

Is it really free to start?
Yes. No credit card required. You get 3 full resume generations free — upgrade only when you need more.
Will recruiters know AI helped write my resume?
CVAI works from your actual experience and the specific job description, so the result reads like your history — not a generic template. You edit everything before it goes out, and the words that ship are the ones you approved.
Does it work for any industry?
Yes. Tech, finance, marketing, healthcare, design — the tone and keywords adapt to whatever job description you paste in.
What is ATS and why does it matter?
An Applicant Tracking System is the software many employers use to filter applications before a person reads them. If your resume doesn't reflect the language of the posting, it can be screened out early. CVAI's ATS analysis shows your match score and what to change.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — cancel in one click. No contracts and no cancellation fees.
