// FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before you start.

Is it really free to start?
Yes. No credit card required. You get 3 full generations completely free — each one includes the main letter, 2 alternative versions, ATS score, and recruiter risk analysis. Upgrade only when you need more.
Will recruiters know AI wrote my letter?
No. The AI uses your actual CV and the specific job description to write a personalized letter — not a generic template. The output sounds like you, tailored to the company. Most recruiters can't tell the difference, and honestly, they don't care as long as the letter is good.
Does it work for any industry or job?
Yes. Tech, finance, marketing, healthcare, design — it works for any industry. The AI adapts the tone and keywords based on the job description you paste. Software engineer applying to Google or a nurse applying to a hospital — same quality result.
What is ATS and why does it matter?
ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System — software that 98% of Fortune 500 companies use to automatically filter applications before a human ever sees them. If your letter doesn't match the right keywords, it gets rejected automatically. Our ATS analysis tells you your score and exactly what to fix.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime with one click. No contracts, no cancellation fees. If you land your job and don't need the service anymore — cancel. Simple as that.
