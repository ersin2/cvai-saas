How does CVAI pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)?
CVAI generates true vector PDFs through ReportLab Platypus rather than canvas screenshot conversions. Text is selectable, searchable, and structured with clean single/double column streams that ATS parsers extract without scrambled text.
Can I upload my existing resume PDF?
Yes! Simply upload your PDF in the Studio or paste text. Our Claude-powered AI pipeline extracts your contact information, experience, education, and skills into editable form fields in under 3 seconds.
What is Google's XYZ formula?
Google's XYZ formula states: "Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". CVAI's section rewriter automatically identifies your duties and structures them into quantified achievement bullets favored by top-tier hiring managers.
